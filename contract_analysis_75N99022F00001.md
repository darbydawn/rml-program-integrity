## City of Hamilton -- Conflict of Interest Flag

The City of Hamilton simultaneously:

- Has local regulatory relationships with the campus
- Is financially dependent on RML ($105M annual personal income flowing through community)

This creates a documented financial relationship between the municipal government
and the federal project it also has regulatory oversight responsibilities for.
